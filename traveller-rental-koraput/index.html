Traveller rental in Koraput, with driver

One 17-seater AC Traveller, a driver who knows Koraput's roads and a support number that answers. This page is the practical part: what you get, what it costs to ask, and how a booking works.

Capacity17 passengers
PricingPer trip, quoted on WhatsApp

What you get

  • A brand-new Force Traveller (September 2026), air conditioned, 17 passenger seats. See the vehicle page for photographs and seating.
  • An experienced local driver for the whole trip, including early Deomali starts.
  • A support contact on WhatsApp from booking to drop-off, for timings, changes and local questions.
  • Trip planning: we suggest the route for your days before you decide.

How the quote works

We quote per trip for an agreed route and number of days, not per kilometre. The quote lists what is included so nothing changes at the end. Typically it covers the Traveller, driver, fuel, tolls and parking for the agreed route. Meals, entry tickets and hotels are paid by you directly. Extra stops beyond the plan are agreed on WhatsApp before the day, with their cost.

We do not publish a rate card because a Deomali day and a Duduma day cost differently. Send dates, group size and route and you get a written price the same day.

Pickup and drop

  • Koraput town: railway station, bus stand or your hotel.
  • Jeypore: town, bus stand or the airport when flights operate.
  • Semiliguda: our base, on the highway between Koraput and Sunabeda.
  • Visakhapatnam or Araku: on request for the full trip; the road via Araku takes five to six hours.

Luggage, seats and driving hours

  • Seventeen people with one small bag each fit comfortably. With large suitcases, 12 to 14 travellers is realistic. Tell us your luggage and we advise honestly.
  • Front rows and window seats for anyone who gets car-sick on ghat roads.
  • We plan long hill drives for daylight and keep the driver's day sensible. Sunrise starts are fine; late-night ghat driving is avoided.

Booking, changes and payment

  1. Day 1
    Share your trip

    Dates, group size, where you arrive and the places you want. WhatsApp is easiest.

  2. Day 2
    Confirm the plan and price

    We send the route, timings and the written quote. Ask anything before you decide.

  3. Day 3
    Pay the booking advance

    The UPI QR and a booking reference come privately on WhatsApp. The advance amount and change terms are written in your quote.

Changes to dates or route are handled on WhatsApp as early as you can tell us. Weather and road conditions in the hills sometimes change a day's plan; the driver and support team will suggest the safe alternative.

Why groups choose one Traveller over three cars

One pickup, one route, one set of timings, nobody waiting for the last car at every viewpoint, and one driver who explains the same thing to everyone at the same time. The group tour guide goes into how a day with 12 to 17 people actually runs.
