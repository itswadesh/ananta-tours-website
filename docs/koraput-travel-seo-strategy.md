# Koraput Group Travel Website — SEO & Content Strategy

## 1. Business Goal

Build a website that attracts tourists from outside Koraput who are planning a Koraput trip, especially groups of approximately 8–17 people.

The website should not depend only on people searching specifically for a vehicle. It should capture travelers while they are researching:

- Koraput tourist places
- Koraput trip planning
- Koraput itineraries
- Koraput tours from Bhubaneswar
- Koraput tours from Kolkata
- Group travel in Koraput
- Local transportation in Koraput

Then convert those visitors into enquiries for our AC Traveller.

---

## 2. Core SEO Strategy

### Important principle

Most tourists will NOT begin their search with:

> "17 seater traveller in Koraput"

They are more likely to begin with searches such as:

- Koraput tourist places
- places to visit in Koraput
- Koraput tour
- Koraput trip
- Koraput itinerary
- Koraput 2 day itinerary
- Koraput 3 day itinerary
- Koraput travel guide
- Koraput sightseeing
- Koraput tour package
- Koraput group tour
- how to travel around Koraput
- Bhubaneswar to Koraput trip
- Kolkata to Koraput trip

Therefore, the website should first help visitors **plan their Koraput trip**, and then naturally introduce the Traveller as the convenient way for their group to explore Koraput.

---

## 3. Target Customer

Primary customer:

- Tourist from outside Koraput
- Coming from Bhubaneswar, Kolkata or other cities
- Traveling with family, friends, colleagues or another group
- Approximately 8–17 travelers
- Wants sightseeing and local transportation
- May not know Koraput well
- Needs help deciding where to go and how to travel between destinations

The website should answer:

1. What should we see in Koraput?
2. How many days do we need?
3. How do we reach Koraput?
4. How do 10–15 people travel together?
5. Which destinations can we cover each day?
6. Can somebody local help us?
7. Is the vehicle comfortable?
8. Can we get a driver/local support?
9. How do we enquire/book?

## 10. Hero Video Behavior

The hero should load quickly.

Recommended implementation:

1. Immediately render optimized hero image.
2. Load video asynchronously in parallel.
3. Do NOT block first paint waiting for video.
4. Track whether visitor remains on/near the hero.
5. If visitor stays for more than approximately 3 seconds and video is ready:
   - transition from image to video
   - autoplay
   - muted
   - loop
   - playsinline
6. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
7. On slow connections/mobile conditions where video would hurt UX, retain the static image.

Use a dark gradient/shadow overlay so headline and CTA remain readable.

## 12. Content Funnel

The SEO funnel should be:

```text
Google Search
      ↓
Koraput Travel Information
      ↓
Destination / Itinerary
      ↓
Visitor starts planning trip
      ↓
"How will our group travel?"
      ↓
One AC Traveller
      ↓
Driver + Local Support
      ↓
WhatsApp / Trip Enquiry
```

This is much stronger than:

```text
Google
↓
Vehicle Rental Page
↓
Book Now
```

because it captures visitors earlier in their travel planning.

## 14. Content Quality Rules

The website should become genuinely useful for somebody who has never visited Koraput.

Do not create dozens of shallow AI SEO pages.

Every page should answer a real travel question.

Use:

- Original information
- Local knowledge
- Real vehicle photography
- Original destination photos where possible
- Maps
- Distances
- Realistic itineraries
- Frequently asked questions
- Local practical tips
- Strong internal linking

Verify routes, opening/access conditions, travel times and seasonal information before publishing.

## 17. SEO Priority

Prioritize in this order:

**Travel discovery intent → itinerary intent → group travel intent → vehicle rental intent → enquiry**

The objective is to rank whenever a relevant prospective tourist is researching a Koraput trip — not only when they already know they need a Traveller.
